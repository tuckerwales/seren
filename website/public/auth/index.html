Seren Auth

A modern, fast and easy to use authenticator for two-factor sign in codes. No network permission at all, setup keys locked with a hardware-backed key, and easy to leave whenever you like.

Features

Everything it does

Common actions are one tap away. Advanced options live in menus and settings, never removed.

Codes

  • Every common kindTime based (TOTP) and counter based (HOTP) codes, SHA-1, SHA-256 or SHA-512, 6 to 8 digits, any period, and Steam Guard.
  • Easy to read outLarge JetBrains Mono digits in groups, with a countdown ring for each account.
  • Tap to copyThe clipboard hides the code from previews and clears it after a minute.
  • No last second rushThe next code appears under the current one in its final seconds.
  • Hidden until you tapOptional, for when people are looking over your shoulder. Search by service or account.

Adding accounts

  • Scan a QR codeWith the camera, or from a screenshot or photo already on your phone.
  • Paste a setup key or linkA whole otpauth link fills in every field, with a live code to check against the site.
  • Spotted on the clipboardCopied a setup link in another app? Come back and a card offers to add it in one tap. You can turn this off.
  • Bring everything overScan Google Authenticator's transfer codes, or import from Aegis, andOTP or a list of links.
  • No duplicatesAccounts are matched by their setup key and never added twice.

Backups and moving devices

  • Encrypted backupsA documented JSON file, encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your password with scrypt.
  • Plain exportA list of otpauth links that most authenticator apps can import.
  • Show a QR codeAdd any account to another device by scanning it from this one.

Privacy and security

  • Offline by designNo network permission at all. Codes come from the time and the setup key.
  • Hardware-backedSetup keys are encrypted with an Android Keystore key and kept out of cloud backups.
  • Screenshots blockedOn by default, which also hides your codes in recent apps.
  • App lockBiometrics or your screen lock, asked for again before a setup key is shown or anything is exported.

Privacy

Codes that never touch the internet

An authenticator only needs the time and a setup key. Seren Auth has no network permission, so your accounts stay on your phone and in the backups you make.

Permissions

  • Network (not used)Not requested. Seren Auth can't send anything anywhere.
  • CameraOnly to read QR codes. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • BiometricsOnly used if you turn on app lock.

Better together

Works with the other Seren apps

Seren Auth works fine on its own. Install the others and the right actions appear.

  • One backup lock

    Seren SSH and Seren Auth protect backups with the same scrypt and AES-256-GCM encryption.

Each action appears only when the other app is installed. The apps hand each other files through ordinary Android intents, never secrets.
