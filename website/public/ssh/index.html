Seren SSH

A modern, fast and easy to use SSH client for Android. A terminal that gets the details right, keys that never leave your phone, and sessions that stay alive while you do something else.

  • Ready for daily use
  • Keys encrypted on device
  • No account

Features

Everything it does

Common actions are one tap away. Advanced options live in menus and settings, never removed.

Terminal

  • A real xterm256 colors and truecolor, bold, italic and underline, mouse reporting, bracketed paste and the alternate screen, so tmux, vim and htop look right.
  • Every character in its placeCJK, emoji and accents line up, and box drawing is pixel perfect in the bundled JetBrains Mono.
  • Touch that makes sensePinch to zoom, fling through scrollback, and long press to select with handles. Scrollback reflows when you rotate.
  • Extra keysEsc, Tab, sticky Ctrl and Alt, arrows that repeat, Home, End, PgUp, PgDn and F1 to F12. Hardware keyboards work too.
  • 11 color schemesMidnight, Dracula, Catppuccin, Nord, Gruvbox, One Dark, Solarized and more, with an override per host.

Connecting

  • Every way to sign inPasswords, keyboard-interactive prompts for 2FA, and public keys.
  • Keys made or importedEd25519, ECDSA and RSA. Generate them on the phone, or import OpenSSH, PEM and PuTTY keys.
  • Trust on first useHost keys are checked by SHA256 fingerprint, with a clear warning if one ever changes.
  • Jump hosts and forwardingProxyJump chains, plus local, remote and dynamic (SOCKS4, 4a and 5) port forwarding.
  • In-app SSH agentOptional, with ForwardAgent per host. It needs app lock, and is wiped whenever the app locks.

Sessions and files

  • Sessions that stay aliveSeveral at once, kept running in the background with keep-alives and one tap reconnect.
  • SFTP browserBrowse, upload, download, rename, delete, create folders and change permissions.
  • Share to uploadShare files from any app, pick an open session or a saved host, choose the folder, and upload.
  • Quick connectType user@host:port or open an ssh:// link. Hosts can run a startup command like tmux new -A -s main.

Your data

  • Hosts and snippetsGroups, colors and search for your hosts, and snippets you can send to any session.
  • Encrypted on the devicePasswords and private keys use an AES-256-GCM key held in the Android Keystore, and never go into cloud backup.
  • Backups you controlExport hosts and snippets as JSON, or everything, keys included, in a backup locked with your password.
  • App lockBiometrics or your screen lock. While it is on, the app is hidden in recent apps.

Privacy

Your keys never leave your phone

Seren SSH talks to the servers you ask it to, and nothing else. Secrets are encrypted with a hardware-backed key and kept out of cloud backups.

Permissions

  • NetworkTo reach the servers you connect to. Nothing else goes out.
  • NotificationsShows your live sessions, so Android keeps them running.
  • BiometricsOnly used if you turn on app lock.
  • Ads, analytics and crash reporting (not used)None at all.

Better together

Works with the other Seren apps

Seren SSH works fine on its own. Install the others and the right actions appear.

  • Upload with Seren SSH

    Send one file or several to a server over SFTP. Pick the server, pick the folder, done.

  • Import into Seren SSH

    Private keys like id_ed25519, .pem and .ppk go straight to Import key, filled in.

  • Show in Seren Files

    After an SFTP download, jump to the folder it was saved in.

  • One backup lock

    Seren SSH and Seren Auth protect backups with the same scrypt and AES-256-GCM encryption.

Each action appears only when the other app is installed. The apps hand each other files through ordinary Android intents, never secrets.
